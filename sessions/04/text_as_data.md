# Text as data: to go further

Part 1 of Session 4 (sixty years, from word lists to LLMs) draws on the course **Text as Data for the Social Sciences** by **Germain Gauthier** (Bocconi University) and **Philine Widmer** (Paris School of Economics), January 2026. Their slides and notebooks go much further than one hour: start here.

## Lectures (PDF)

| | Lecture |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | [Introduction](https://pinchofdata.github.io/germaingauthier/pdfs/teaching/text%20as%20data/01_introduction.pdf): the research pipeline, bag of words, tf-idf |
| 2 | [Dictionaries](https://pinchofdata.github.io/germaingauthier/pdfs/teaching/text%20as%20data/02_dictionaries.pdf): general lexicons, sentiment, Economic Policy Uncertainty |

| 6 | [Semantics](https://pinchofdata.github.io/germaingauthier/pdfs/teaching/text%20as%20data/06_semantics.pdf): named entities, dependency parsing, semantic roles |
| 7 | [Transformers](https://pinchofdata.github.io/germaingauthier/pdfs/teaching/text%20as%20data/07_transformers.pdf): attention, BERT and GPT, how LLMs are trained |
