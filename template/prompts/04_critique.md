# Prompt 04: hostile reviewer

---

Read `notes/research_memo.md`. You are now Reviewer 2 at a good journal in this field, and you want this paper rejected.

Write `notes/critique_memo.md` with the five most serious weaknesses of the memo, ranked from most to least damaging. For each one: the weakness in one sentence, why it matters in two, and the single change that would address it.

Rules:

- Attack the design and the logic, not the writing.
- Be specific: quote the sentence of the memo you are attacking.
- Do not modify `notes/research_memo.md`. Propose; do not apply. I decide.
- At the end, one line: would you reject, ask for major revisions, or minor revisions, and why.
