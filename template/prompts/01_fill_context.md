# Prompt 01: fill the project context

Paste this as your first message in a fresh agent session, inside the project folder.

---

Read `README.md`, `CLAUDE.md` and `PROJECT_HISTORY.md`. They are templates: most sections are empty.

Your job is to fill `README.md` and the "Conventions of this project" section of `CLAUDE.md` with me. Do not invent anything. Interview me instead:

1. Ask me, one question at a time, what you need to fill each section of `README.md`: research question, design, data, literature, people. Keep each question short. If I answer "skip", leave the section with a one-line placeholder saying what is missing.
2. Then ask me about conventions: the language of the notes, the citation style, the names I use for my main variables, the software I work with (R, Python, Stata), and anything I want you to always do or never do.
3. When you have what you need, write `README.md` and the conventions section of `CLAUDE.md`. Show me the result and ask me to confirm before saving.

Rules: no filler, no praise, no assumptions about my field. If I give you a vague answer, ask one follow-up question, not three.
