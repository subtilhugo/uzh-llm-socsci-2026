# Prompt 02: literature notes

Before pasting: put your PDFs in `literature/`. Three to five papers.

---

There are PDFs in `literature/`. For each one, write a literature note in `notes/literature/`, named `<firstauthor>_<year>.md`, following exactly the structure in `notes/templates/literature_note.md`.

Rules:

- Read the actual PDF. If you cannot open a PDF, tell me which one and stop; do not write a note from the title or from what you think you know about the paper.
- Every number and every finding must come with the page, table or figure it comes from. If you cannot locate it, write "location not found" next to it.
- The section "Link with our project" must refer to the research question in `README.md`. If `README.md` is still empty, say so and leave that section as a question for me.
- Fill "Claims to verify" with the two or three claims that matter most for our project, with the page where I will check them.
- Set "Verified by: not yet verified" in each note. I will change it myself after checking.

When you are done, list the notes you wrote and, for each, the one claim you are least sure about.
