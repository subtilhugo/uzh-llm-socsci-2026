# Literature

The PDFs go in this folder. They are **not** in the public repository (`.gitignore` excludes
`literature/*.pdf`): articles and books are under copyright. Download them through your library.

| File name to use | Reference | Where to get it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `subtil_2026_betting_alone.pdf` | Subtil, H. (2026). *Betting Alone*. Working paper; CEPREMAP note, January 2026. [complete: co-authors, full title] | From Hugo |
| `colantone_stanig_2018.pdf` | Colantone, I. & Stanig, P. (2018). The Trade Origins of Economic Nationalism: Import Competition and Voting Behavior in Western Europe. *American Journal of Political Science*, 62(4), 936–953. | https://doi.org/10.1111/ajps.12358 |
| `autor_et_al_2020.pdf` | Autor, D., Dorn, D., Hanson, G. & Majlesi, K. (2020). Importing Political Polarization? The Electoral Consequences of Rising Trade Exposure. *American Economic Review*, 110(10), 3139–3183. | https://doi.org/10.1257/aer.20170011 |
| `coquard_2019_ch*.pdf` | Coquard, B. (2019). *Ceux qui restent. Faire sa vie dans les campagnes en déclin*. Paris: La Découverte. | Book: one or two chapters as PDF |
| `ternullo_2024_ch*.pdf` | Ternullo, S. (2024). *How the Heartland Went Red: Why Local Forces Matter in an Age of Nationalized Politics*. Princeton: Princeton University Press. | Book: introduction or one chapter as PDF |

For the books, put only the chapters you have read or want the agent to read. A whole book is long
and expensive to process, and a note must say which chapters it is based on.

BibTeX entries: `references.bib`.
